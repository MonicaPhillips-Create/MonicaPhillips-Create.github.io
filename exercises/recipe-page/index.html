APPETIZER / SIDE DISH

WHIPPED EGGPLANT

This whipped eggplant dip is smooth, creamy, and packed with roasted eggplant and caramelized garlic. Inspired by the eggplant dip at Elephante, it’s blended with tahini, crème fraîche, and lemon until light and silky with the perfect balance of tang and richness. I love serving it with pizza dough brushed with olive oil and baked until golden and bubbly, then torn apart for dipping.

PREP TIME:10 MINUTES COOK TIME:50 MINUTES

TOTAL TIME:1 HOUR YIELDS:2 TO 3 CUPS

INGREDIENTS

ROASTED EGGPLANT AND GARLIC
  • 3 medium or 2 large eggplants
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 whole head garlic
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Pinch of salt
FOR THE DIP
  • ⅓ cup crème fraîche
  • 3 tablespoons tahini
  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice
  • ½ teaspoon salt, more to to taste
  • 1/2 tablespoon sugar
  • 1/4 cup olive oil

INSTRUCTIONS

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F.
  2. Slice the eggplants in half lengthwise and score them across diagonally. Place them cut side up on a parchment-lined baking sheet. Drizzle with olive oil and sprinkle with salt.
  3. Slice the top off the whole head of garlic to expose the cloves. Place it on the tray or wrap loosely in foil, drizzle with olive oil, and sprinkle with a pinch of salt.
  4. Roast the eggplant and garlic together for about 40 minutes, flipping the eggplant over halfway. When the eggplant is completely soft and collapsed and the garlic is golden and caramelized, it's done. The garlic may need about 10 to 15 extra minutes if the cloves are still firm.
  5. Let everything cool slightly. Scoop the eggplant flesh out of the skins and transfer it to a food processor. Squeeze the roasted garlic cloves out of their skins and add them in as well.
  6. Add the crème fraîche, tahini, lemon juice, salt, and sugar. Blend until completely smooth.
  7. With the processor running, slowly drizzle in the olive oil and blend until the dip becomes silky, whipped, and airy.
  8. For an extra smooth consistency, you can optionally strain the dip through a fine mesh sieve after blending.
  9. Transfer to a bowl and finish with an extra drizzle of olive oil, flaky sea salt, and fresh cracked black pepper before serving. Serve with warm pita bread or toasted flatbread.
